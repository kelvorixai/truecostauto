Why the monthly payment alone is misleading

Our 5-year total cost of ownership calculator covers what owning a car really costs. This workbook goes further: a lease payment and a loan payment can look similar side by side while hiding very different totals — resale value, excess-mileage penalties, and end-of-term fees all land outside the monthly number. Comparing both scenarios over the same period is the only way to see which one actually costs less.
