How this is calculated

This adds up the five standard inputs behind any total cost of ownership figure, over your chosen ownership period:

  • Depreciation: purchase price minus expected resale value
  • Financing: total interest paid over the period (enter 0 if paying cash)
  • Insurance: annual premium × years
  • Maintenance & repairs: annual estimate × years
  • Fuel or energy: annual cost × years

The total is then divided by your total expected mileage over the period to get a cost-per-mile figure — the number that makes it possible to compare two different vehicles, or different ownership periods, on equal footing.

This is a projection based on the numbers you enter, not a quote — actual costs vary by vehicle, driving habits, and market conditions. It doesn't account for taxes, opportunity cost of cash, or inflation.
