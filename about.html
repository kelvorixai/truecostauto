About TrueCostAuto

A small library of plain-English, data-driven guides on what it actually costs to own and run a car — insurance, energy and fuel, maintenance, depreciation, and how they combine over time.

Most car-cost content online is written to help you decide which car or which policy to buy. TrueCostAuto deliberately isn't that. Every guide here breaks down the mechanics behind a cost figure — why the insurance-by-age curve is shaped the way it is, how an EV's cost per mile is actually built up, how a total-cost-of-ownership number is calculated — using the underlying data and methodology, not a recommendation.

Everything here is general education, not personalized financial or purchasing advice, and not tied to any insurer, dealer, manufacturer, or lender. Figures are illustrative and based on published national averages at the time of writing — real premiums, prices, and rates vary by individual, vehicle, and location. For a number specific to your situation, get a quote or estimate from the relevant insurer, dealer, or lender directly.
